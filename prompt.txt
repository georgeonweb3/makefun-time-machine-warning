Build brief — Funniest Time Machine Warning Label (make.fun, program/v1, FREE, $2 pot)

Task: write ONE one-line warning label for a time machine (max 120
characters) and a Python 3 program (main.py at the repo root) that writes it
to /output/entry.txt — UTF-8, deterministic, no network at run time.

Judge brief (primary spec, from the bounty rules): an AI judge compares
entries two at a time and keeps the funnier one. Criteria, most important
first:
1. It is a time machine warning label (anything short of one loses to any
   funny warning label for a time machine).
2. Turns a real hazard of time travel — a paradox, a duplicate self, a
   stranded ancestor — into the joke instead of naming it flatly.
3. Saves the surprise for the last few words, so the laugh lands where the
   reader least expects it.
4. Keeps a straight face, phrased like a genuine safety notice, so the joke
   cuts against official calm.
5. Wastes no words inside the character limit, with every phrase doing work.

Winner-first: the field at survey (80 entries) is saturated with "do not
meet yourself" variants; the strongest lines already in are "Do not meet
yourself. He has had longer to plan." and "WARNING: Do not kill your
grandfather. He is the only one who knows how this thing works." The entry
must beat those head-to-head, so it needs a different hazard and a
punchline that lands later than theirs.

Work done in this session:
- Drafted 4 candidate labels (A–D), scored each 1–5 against the five judge
  criteria, and picked A — "WARNING: Do not warn your younger self. He
  ignored you then, too." — the only candidate scoring 5 on every
  criterion: the paradox is the joke itself (the warning is the warning your
  younger self ignored), the surprise lands in the last three words, it
  reads as a genuine safety notice, and at 65 chars nothing is wasted.
- Wrote main.py (v1 with the label), then revised/polished it in a later

  "never listened then either" variants, rejected both) and recorded it in
